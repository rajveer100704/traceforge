# v0.2 plan — research integrity and hardening (written before implementation)

Scope from the v0.2 brief; architecture and public contracts preserved (new fields have defaults
and are hash-neutral at their defaults).

1. **Device handling (P0).** New `modules/training/device.py` resolves `auto|cpu|cuda` and
   `auto|fp32|bf16|fp16`, fails closed on unavailable CUDA, and records hardware/software facts.
   SFT moves model + batches to the device with autocast; GRPO derives `use_cpu`/`bf16`/`fp16`
   from the resolved device (removes unconditional `use_cpu=True`). Tests: CPU path, injected
   fake-CUDA torch, real CUDA when present.
2. **Container scanning (P0).** Scan `traceforge:ci` and `traceforge-sandbox:0.1` with pinned
   Trivy v0.70.0; JSON/SARIF/table reports uploaded as artifacts; `scripts/trivy_gate.py` fails
   CRITICAL/HIGH unless a time-bounded exception applies; triage CVE-2026-76642 and
   CVE-2025-69720 (and the rest); harden images (security updates, setuid removal); keep digests
   pinned and verify non-root in CI.
3. **Statistics (P0).** Keep Wilson; add task-clustered bootstrap, task-level summaries,
   `n_tasks` vs `n_episodes`, repeated seeds, threshold sensitivity, evidence labels.
4. **Fail-closed integrity (P0).** Lineage checks (task/template/fixture), training-overlap checks
   against declared training lineage, refusal of official runs (diagnostic mode explicit), full
   leakage report persisted; SFT/GRPO guards; negative tests.
5. **Experiments F, G, H (P1)** and **reproducibility bundles (P1)** for every experiment, with
   NOT EXECUTED arms where no pretrained model/endpoint exists.
6. Docs (protocols, ADR-011..013, status, technical report), full local verification, PR.
